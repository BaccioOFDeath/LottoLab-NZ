How we calculate and compare these odds +

Our theoretical probabilities use the hypergeometric distribution: P(k matches) = C(20,k) × C(60,n−k) / C(80,n), where n is the number selected. Historical figures count results for a fixed, predeclared reference line or retrospectively for the line you selected. A 95% Wilson interval shows sampling uncertainty. The payout categories are defined by the current NZ Keno rules. Different historical percentages do not establish better chances in future independent random draws.
