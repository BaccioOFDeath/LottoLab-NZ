# LottoLab NZ — competitor audit and product direction
Date researched: 2026-10-25
Competitor audited: http://lottoresults.co.nz/ (HTTPS www endpoint was not reliably accessible during audit)
LottoLab: https://baccioofdeath.github.io/LottoLab-NZ/

## Visitor intent hierarchy
1. See latest Lotto / Bonus / Powerball results, with draw number, date and source confidence.
2. Find a clearly labelled next-draw set of **20 pre-recorded research lines** and copy them easily.
3. Judge how past predictions performed, using actual post-publication scoring separate from historical backtests and a random control.
4. Only then explore archival draws, frequencies, geography, statistics and number worlds.

## Competitor advantages established by accessible pages
- Results for Lotto, Keno, Bullseye and Play 3, and international jackpot previews.
- Draw detail pages provide per-division winners, payouts and match conditions.
- A historical archive with individual draw pages (including results from 2008).
- Nine Lotto tools: generator, number analysis, checker, common pairs, most-drawn, triplets, last-seen, skip-and-hit, and number buddies.
- A checker capable of comparing a user's numbers against historical results.

## Competitor limitations observed on 2026-10-25
- Home page Lotto latest reported date was 2026-09-12, though Keno and Bullseye were more recent. Do not interpret that as proof the operator's backend has no newer Lotto draws.
- Home page Play 3 result was dated 2019-03-02.
- Lotto game guide described the pre-September-2026 ten-ball Powerball rules; rule history must be labelled.
- Nine tools are distributed across separate pages, adding navigation cost.
- Some claims about number-generation strategy cannot be treated as validated prediction advantage.

## Improvements implemented in October 2026 Studio V2
- Shift to a constrained, clean navy / teal / slate visual system, sharper borders, mobile navigation dock, reduced visual density.
- Results-first small-screen presentation; preserve actual draw dates and no fake live prize figures.
- Next draw: 20 immutable research lines stay centrally accessible without unlimited reroll.
- Transparent performance summary of real prospective draws and historical backtest model-vs-random averages.
- Optional six-number latest-draw comparison. Not a ticket checker or a prize claim.
- Progressive Advanced Lab disclosure with auto-open on deep links, preserving number archive, Geo Lab, Number World, Universe, evidence and exports.
- Quick links to results, lines, performance, and deeper tools.

## Gaps — do not claim feature parity until resolved
P0: Full archive coverage and independent verification of historical draws, including draw-era rules; monitor ingestion health in actual scheduled runs.
P0: Reliable post-draw updates and verified result freshness; stale data must be visibly dated, never silently treated as current.
P0: Observe actual future prospective scoring before making predictive performance claims.
P1: Prize-division payouts and number of winners; requires verified, legally reusable source data.
P1: Full historical number checker by draw date / draw ID, including official payout lookup only if reliably sourced.
P1: Ordered Strike results and independent game coverage for Keno/Bullseye if expanding beyond Lotto.
P2: Date-range archive navigation, more accessibility/browser/performance checks, shareable result links.
P2: Last-seen/buddies/triplets tools only if they have clear interpretive value. No fabricated 'hot numbers win more' claims.

## Non-negotiables
- Never promise a winning-number or location prediction edge for a fair independent draw.
- Never mix Bonus forecasts into playable ticket line text.
- Never claim historical backtests are prospective proof.
- Never label a source as 'officially audited' from two independent third-party archives.
- Respect reduced motion, touch scrolling, keyboard navigation, constrained mobile widths, and data-source failures.
