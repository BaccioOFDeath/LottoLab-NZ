How we measure success

We record six main numbers and Powerball for each model before the draw, then score main-number matches (0–6) and Powerball separately after both sources confirm the result. We evaluate on unseen historical draws and compare against random picks. A fair 6-of-40 line averages 0.9 main-number matches per draw.
